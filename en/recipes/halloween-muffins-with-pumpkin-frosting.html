Halloween Muffins with Pumpkin Frosting

Make this Halloween Muffins with Pumpkin Frosting with Sweet Protein Chocolate: an easy recipe you can have ready in about 25 minutes. It includes 4 eggs, 60 ml of hot water and 200 g of vegetable oil and is perfect for an afternoon snack or a dessert. Sweet Protein Chocolate provides 20 g of protein per 100 g of finished product.

7 ingredients5 steps25 min approx.
Made withSweet Protein Chocolate500 gView product

Ingredients

  • 500 g of Sweet Protein Chocolate
  • 4 eggs
  • 60 ml of hot water
  • 200 g of vegetable oil
  • Cinnamon (optional)
  • 4 tablespoons of roasted pumpkin
  • 3 tablespoons of mascarpone
Nutrition informationSpiced muffins without processed sweets

Method

  1. 01Combine the Sweet Protein Chocolate mix with the eggs, hot water, oil and cinnamon
  2. 02Bake at 160 °C for 15 minutes
  3. 03Leave to cool
  4. 04Mix the roasted pumpkin with the mascarpone until smooth
  5. 05Top the muffins with the frosting

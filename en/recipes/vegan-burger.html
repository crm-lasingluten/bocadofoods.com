Vegan Burger

Vegan Burger: an easy idea with Plant-Based Mince Mix for lunch or dinner, in about 15 minutes. It includes low Carb burger bun, 200 ml of water and vegetables of your choice. Plant-Based Mince Mix provides 11.4 g of protein per 100 g of finished product.

8 ingredients6 steps15 min approx.
Made withPlant-Based Mince Mix 150 g150 g · makes 450 gView product

Ingredients

  • Low Carb burger bun
  • 100 g of plant-based mince mix
  • 200 ml of water
  • Vegetables of your choice
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Ground cumin
  • Mayonnaise or toppings of your choice
Nutrition information1 burger: 30 g protein, less than 2 g sugars, 11 g fibre

Method

  1. 01Chop the vegetables and cook them in a frying pan with olive oil, salt and cumin
  2. 02Mix 100 g of plant-based mince with 200 ml of water and the cooked vegetables
  3. 03Mix until combined and chill for 30 minutes to hydrate
  4. 04Shape the mixture into 2 burgers
  5. 05Toast the Low Carb burger bun
  6. 06Assemble with your chosen toppings

Berry Cheesecake with Toasts

Make this Berry Cheesecake with Toasts with Low Carb Toasts: an easy recipe you can have ready in about 10 minutes. It includes nut butter, yoghurt and cream cheese and is perfect for an afternoon snack or a dessert. Low Carb Toasts provides 44 g of protein per 100 g.

8 ingredients4 steps10 min approx.With video
Made withLow Carb Toasts125 gView product

Ingredients

  • Low Carb Toasts
  • Nut butter
  • Yoghurt
  • Cream cheese
  • Vanilla essence
  • Sweetener
  • Lemon juice
  • Frozen berries

Method

  1. 01Blitz the toasts with nut butter for the base
  2. 02Mix yoghurt, cream cheese and vanilla essence for the filling
  3. 03Make a berry jam by reducing the berries with sweetener and lemon
  4. 04Assemble the cheesecake in layers

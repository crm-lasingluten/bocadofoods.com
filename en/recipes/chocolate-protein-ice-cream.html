Chocolate Protein Ice Cream

Chocolate Protein Ice Cream: a very simple idea with Protein Choco Spread for an afternoon snack or a dessert, in about 10 minutes. It includes 200 g of cottage cheese.

2 ingredients3 steps10 min approx.

Ingredients

  • 200 g of cottage cheese
  • 200 g of Protein Choco Spread
Nutrition information28 g protein per ice cream, very low in carbohydrate

Method

  1. 01Blend both ingredients together
  2. 02Pour into ice-lolly moulds
  3. 03Freeze

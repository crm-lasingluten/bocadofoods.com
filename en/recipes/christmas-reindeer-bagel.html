Christmas Reindeer Bagel

Christmas Reindeer Bagel is a very simple recipe with Protein Bagel, ready in about 10 minutes. It includes peanut butter, banana and strawberry and is perfect for an afternoon snack or a dessert. Protein Bagel provides 27 g of protein per 100 g.

6 ingredients3 steps10 min approx.
Made withProtein Bagel240 g · 3 × 80 gView product

Ingredients

  • 1 Protein Bagel Soft
  • Peanut butter
  • Banana
  • Strawberry
  • Blueberries
  • Almonds
Nutrition informationHigh-protein festive breakfast

Method

  1. 01Spread peanut butter on both halves of the bagel
  2. 02Add banana slices and close
  3. 03Decorate as a reindeer: strawberry nose, eyes of peanut butter and blueberries, almond antlers

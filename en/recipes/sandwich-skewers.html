Sandwich Skewers

Sandwich Skewers is an easy recipe with The Original Protein Bread, ready in about 10 minutes. It includes cheese, ham and tomato and is perfect for lunch, a light dinner or a packed meal. The Original Protein Bread provides 28 g of protein per 100 g.

5 ingredients4 steps10 min approx.With video
Made withThe Original Protein Bread500 gView product

Ingredients

  • The Original Protein Bread
  • Cheese
  • Ham
  • Tomato
  • Olive
Nutrition information2 slices (62 g): 3 g carbohydrate, 17 g protein, 9 g fibre, GI 24

Method

  1. 01Make a ham and cheese toastie
  2. 02Cut into 4
  3. 03Thread onto skewers, alternating with tomato
  4. 04Finish with an olive

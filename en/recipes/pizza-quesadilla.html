Pizza Quesadilla

Pizza Quesadilla is an easy recipe with Low Carb Wrap, ready in about 25 minutes. It includes fresh tomato, courgette and cooked ham or turkey slices and is perfect for lunch or dinner. Low Carb Wrap provides 22 g of protein per 100 g.

5 ingredients4 steps25 min approx.With video
Made withLow Carb Wrap320 g · 8 × 40 g tortillasView product

Ingredients

  • BOCADO low carb wraps
  • Fresh tomato
  • Courgette
  • Cooked ham or turkey slices
  • Grated cheese
Nutrition information1 tortilla (40 g): 14 g carbohydrate, 9 g protein, 3 g fibre

Method

  1. 01Put the toppings on a base wrap: tomato, courgette, ham and cheese
  2. 02Cover with another wrap with tomato on both sides
  3. 03Top with more grated cheese
  4. 04Air fryer for 15 min at 170 °C

Sweet Protein Cookies

Make this Sweet Protein Cookies with Sweet Protein Chocolate: an easy recipe you can have ready in about 25 minutes. It includes 1 tablespoon of peanut butter, 1 drizzle of extra virgin olive oil (optional) and 6 tablespoons of milk and is perfect for an afternoon snack or a dessert. Sweet Protein Chocolate provides 20 g of protein per 100 g of finished product.

4 ingredients4 steps25 min approx.
Made withSweet Protein Chocolate500 gView product

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of Sweet Protein Chocolate mix
  • 1 tablespoon of peanut butter
  • 1 drizzle of extra virgin olive oil (optional)
  • 6 tablespoons of milk
Nutrition informationAlmost 40 g protein, no added sugar, high in fibre and healthy fats

Method

  1. 01Combine all the ingredients with a spatula, then with your hands
  2. 02Shape the cookies
  3. 03Bake for 8 minutes at 200 °C
  4. 04Optional: dip in chocolate

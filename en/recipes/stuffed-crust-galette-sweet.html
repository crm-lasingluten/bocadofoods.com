Stuffed-Crust Galette (Sweet)

Stuffed-Crust Galette (Sweet): an easy idea with Low Carb Wrap for an afternoon snack or a dessert, in about 15 minutes. It includes cheese, tomato or filling of your choice and egg. Low Carb Wrap provides 22 g of protein per 100 g.

4 ingredients4 steps15 min approx.
Made withLow Carb Wrap320 g · 8 × 40 g tortillasView product

Ingredients

  • Low Carb Wraps
  • Cheese
  • Tomato or filling of your choice
  • Egg
Nutrition information1 tortilla (40 g): 14 g carbohydrate, 9 g protein, 2.5 g fibre

Method

  1. 01Place small pieces of cheese around the edge of the wrap
  2. 02Roll the edges over and secure with cocktail sticks
  3. 03Fill as you like (tomato and egg)
  4. 04Cook in a frying pan

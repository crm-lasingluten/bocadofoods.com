Salmon Wrap Bites

Salmon Wrap Bites is an easy recipe with Low Carb Wrap, ready in about 15 minutes. It includes egg, cheese and rocket and is perfect for breakfast or brunch. Low Carb Wrap provides 18 g of protein per 100 g.

6 ingredients4 steps15 min approx.
Made withLow Carb Wrap320 g · 8 × 40 g tortillasView product

Ingredients

  • Low Carb Wrap
  • Egg
  • Cheese
  • Rocket
  • Lamb’s lettuce
  • Smoked salmon
Nutrition information1 tortilla (40 g): 16 g carbohydrate, 7.2 g protein, 2.4 g fibre

Method

  1. 01Beat two eggs into a hot frying pan
  2. 02Place a wrap on top and cook
  3. 03Fill with cheese, rocket, lamb’s lettuce and salmon
  4. 04Roll up and slice

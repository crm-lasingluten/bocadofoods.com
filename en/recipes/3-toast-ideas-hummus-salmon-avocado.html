3 Toast Ideas (Hummus, Salmon, Avocado)

3 Toast Ideas (Hummus, Salmon, Avocado): a very simple idea with Low Carb Protein Rolls for breakfast or brunch, in about 25 minutes. It includes cherry tomatoes, salmon and cream cheese. Low Carb Protein Rolls provides 29 g of protein per 100 g.

8 ingredients3 steps25 min approx.
Made withLow Carb Protein Rolls350 g · 7 × 50 gView product

Ingredients

  • Bocado protein rolls
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Salmon
  • Cream cheese
  • Basil
  • Walnuts
  • Avocado
  • Quail eggs
Nutrition information1 roll (50 g): 6.5 g carbohydrate, 14.5 g protein, 4.1 g fibre

Method

  1. 01Roast cherry tomatoes with oil and spices for the first toast, with hummus
  2. 02Blend cream cheese with basil and walnuts for the second, with salmon
  3. 03Spread avocado and top with quail eggs for the third

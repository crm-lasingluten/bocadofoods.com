Cheese-Stuffed Veggie Burgers

Make this Cheese-Stuffed Veggie Burgers with Plant-Based Mince Mix: an easy recipe you can have ready in about 15 minutes. It includes asparagus, carrot and cheese and is perfect for lunch or dinner. Plant-Based Mince Mix provides 11.4 g of protein per 100 g of finished product.

4 ingredients5 steps15 min approx.With video
Made withPlant-Based Mince Mix 150 g150 g · makes 450 gView product

Ingredients

  • Bocado plant-based mince
  • Asparagus
  • Carrot
  • Cheese
Nutrition informationHigh in protein, low in fat, source of fibre, cholesterol-free, gluten-free, suitable for vegans

Method

  1. 01Sauté the asparagus and carrot
  2. 02Hydrate the plant-based mince
  3. 03Mix with the sautéed vegetables
  4. 04Shape into burgers stuffed with cheese
  5. 05Cook in a frying pan

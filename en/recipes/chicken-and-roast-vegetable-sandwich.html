Chicken and Roast Vegetable Sandwich

Chicken and Roast Vegetable Sandwich is an easy recipe with Low Carb Baguettes, ready in about 15 minutes. It includes guacamole, chicken breast and roasted vegetables and is perfect for lunch, a light dinner or a packed meal. Low Carb Baguettes provides 27 g of protein per 100 g.

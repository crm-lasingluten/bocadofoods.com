Salmon, Cheese and Rocket Pizza

Salmon, Cheese and Rocket Pizza: a very simple idea with Protein Pizza Mix for lunch or dinner, in about 25 minutes. It includes mozzarella cheese, salmon and brie cheese. Protein Pizza Mix provides 27.3 g of protein per 100 g of finished product.

Description

A mix for making plant-based mince from pea protein, 100% vegan and gluten-free, in a 500 g pack. It yields three times its weight in mince ready to cook: burgers, meatballs, bolognese, tacos or fillings. Just hydrate, let it rest and shape.

Ingredients and allergens

Protein mix (49%) (textured pea protein), extruded rice, methylcellulose; maize semolina, rapeseed oil, salt, sunflower seed flour, spices (cumin, coriander), beetroot juice powder; instant caramel powder, herbs, smoke flavouring, parsnip powder, citric acid; natural flavouring, onion powder.

Allergens: Contains no allergens subject to mandatory declaration.

Description

Low Carb High Protein Rolls: 7 rolls of 50 g with 29 g of protein and 8.2 g of fibre per 100 g. Their size is perfect for sandwiches, homemade burgers or as a side with meals. Made with a blend of wheat, soya and lupin proteins, they are an easy way to add protein to any meal of the day.

Ingredients and allergens

Protein blend (35%) (wheat protein, soya protein, lupin protein), soya flour, wheat flour, wholemeal wheat flour, soya semolina, soya bran, sugar, oat fibre, salt, emulsifier: sunflower lecithin; acidity regulator: sodium diacetate; preservative: calcium propionate; calcium carbonate; inactive yeast.

Allergens: Contains gluten from wheat and oats, soya and lupin. May contain traces of barley, rye, spelt, egg and milk.

Description

One ingredient, countless recipes. Sweet Protein Chocolate is a mix for making chocolate brownies, muffins, sponge cakes and pancakes with 20 g of protein per 100 g of prepared product. It contains fat-reduced cocoa and a blend of whey, milk, wheat and egg protein.

Ingredients and allergens

Protein blend (31%) (whey protein concentrate (emulsifier: lecithin E322 (sunflower)), wheat protein, sunflower seed flour, milk protein (emulsifier: lecithin E322 (sunflower)), egg white powder), fructose, oat fibre, collagen hydrolysate (beef), fat-reduced cocoa powder (8%), wheat bran, raising agents: disodium diphosphate E450(i), sodium hydrogen carbonate E500(ii); emulsifiers: mono- and diglycerides and polyglycerol esters of fatty acids E471 and E475; flavouring, salt, preservative: potassium sorbate E202; sweetener: steviol glycosides E960; thickener: xanthan E415.

Allergens: Contains milk, egg and gluten from wheat and oats. May contain traces of barley, rye, spelt, soya and lupin.

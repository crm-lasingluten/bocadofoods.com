Description

Small, crunchy toasts made from our protein bread. With 44 g of protein and 19 g of fibre per 100 g, they are a snack between meals, a side for soups and salads, or a base for pâtés and cheese spreads.

Ingredients and allergens

Protein blend (35%) (wheat protein, sunflower seed flour, soya protein), wholemeal wheat flour (17%), soya semolina (12%), wheat bran (10%), flax seeds (7%), soya flour (6%), linseed flour (5%), oat fibre (5%), salt (<2%), emulsifier (<2%): soya lecithin; acidity regulator (<2%): sodium diacetate; water and yeast.

Allergens: Contains gluten from wheat and oats, and soya. May contain traces of barley, rye, spelt, egg, milk, hazelnuts, sesame and lupin.

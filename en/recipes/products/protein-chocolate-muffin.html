Description

A ready-to-eat chocolate muffin with 20% protein. It comes in a 50 g individual pack, easy to carry in your bag, backpack or gym bag, and is a sweet snack with more protein.

Ingredients and allergens

Egg (19%), sorbitol* (13%), oil (11%), protein blend (11%) (whey protein concentrate (milk), emulsifier: lecithin (E 322, sunflower), wheat protein, milk protein concentrate, egg white powder), oat fibre, collagen hydrolysate (beef), fat-reduced cocoa powder, sunflower seed flour, wheat bran, yeast, raising agents: disodium diphosphate (E 450 (i)), sodium hydrogen carbonate (E 500 (ii)); emulsifiers: mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids (E 471), polyglycerol esters of fatty acids (E 475); flavouring, thickener: xanthan (E 415).
*Excessive consumption may produce laxative effects.

Allergens: Contains egg, milk and gluten from wheat and oats.

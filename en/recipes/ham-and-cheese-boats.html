Ham and Cheese Boats

Ham and Cheese Boats: an easy idea with Low Carb Protein Rolls for an afternoon snack or a dessert, in about 25 minutes. It includes 1 egg, cooked ham and cheese. Low Carb Protein Rolls provides 29 g of protein per 100 g.

5 ingredients4 steps25 min approx.
Made withLow Carb Protein Rolls350 g · 7 × 50 gView product

Ingredients

  • IG24 Rolls
  • 1 egg
  • Cooked ham
  • Cheese
  • Oregano or basil (optional)
Nutrition informationHigh in protein, very low in carbohydrate

Method

  1. 01Beat the egg with the ham and cheese
  2. 02Make a cut in the roll to fill it
  3. 03Fill the roll
  4. 04Bake at 200 °C for 15 minutes

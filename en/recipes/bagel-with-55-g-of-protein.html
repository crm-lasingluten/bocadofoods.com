Bagel with 55 g of Protein

Bagel with 55 g of Protein: an easy idea with Protein Bagel for breakfast or brunch, in about 15 minutes. It includes 2 eggs, 2 slices of cheddar cheese and 40 g of turkey. Protein Bagel provides 27 g of protein per 100 g.

5 ingredients4 steps15 min approx.
Made withProtein Bagel240 g · 3 × 80 gView product

Ingredients

  • 1 Protein Bagel
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 40 g of turkey
  • 1/2 tomato
Nutrition information55 g protein, less than 10 g carbohydrate, 25 g fibre

Method

  1. 01Slice the bagel and toast it (optional)
  2. 02Beat the eggs and cook in a frying pan like an omelette
  3. 03Add the cheddar to the omelette and fold
  4. 04Assemble the bagel with the egg, cheese, turkey and sliced tomato

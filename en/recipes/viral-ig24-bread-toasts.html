Viral IG24 Bread Toasts

Make this Viral IG24 Bread Toasts with IG24 Bread: an easy recipe you can have ready in about 15 minutes. It includes 2 egg whites, 2 whole eggs and avocado and is perfect for breakfast or brunch. IG24 Bread provides 28 g of protein per 100 g.

5 ingredients4 steps15 min approx.
Made withIG24 Bread500 gView product

Ingredients

  • 2 egg whites
  • 2 whole eggs
  • 2 slices of IG24 Bread
  • Avocado
  • Tomato
Nutrition information37 g protein. 2 slices of IG24 Bread: 17 g protein, 3 g carbs, 9 g fibre

Method

  1. 01Beat the egg whites and eggs and make an omelette in a hot frying pan
  2. 02Place the 2 slices of bread on top of the omelette
  3. 03Flip to finish cooking
  4. 04Add avocado and tomato before folding and serving

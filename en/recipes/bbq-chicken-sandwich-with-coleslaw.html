BBQ Chicken Sandwich with Coleslaw

BBQ Chicken Sandwich with Coleslaw is an easy recipe with Burger Bun Mix, ready in about 15 minutes. It includes boneless, skinless chicken thigh, BBQ sauce with no added sugar and ground cumin and is perfect for lunch or dinner. Burger Bun Mix provides 29 g of protein per 100 g of finished product.

14 ingredients5 steps15 min approx.
Made withBurger Bun Mix1 kgView product

Ingredients

  • Low Carb burger bun
  • Boneless, skinless chicken thigh
  • BBQ sauce with no added sugar
  • Ground cumin
  • Agave
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Grated red apple
  • Grated cucumber
  • Grated carrot
  • Grated beetroot
  • Light mayonnaise
  • Lemon juice
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt
Nutrition informationReady in 15 minutes

Method

  1. 01Marinate the chicken with BBQ sauce, cumin and agave
  2. 02Grill until tender and juicy
  3. 03Mix the light mayonnaise with lemon juice, salt, agave and extra virgin olive oil
  4. 04Combine with the grated vegetables to make the coleslaw
  5. 05Assemble the sandwich with the bun, chicken and coleslaw, with melted cheese

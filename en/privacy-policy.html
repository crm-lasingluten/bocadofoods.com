If you believe we have not handled your rights correctly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

The services of this website are not aimed at children under 14. If you are under that age, do not provide us with personal data without the consent of your parents or guardians.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration, in accordance with Article 32 GDPR.

Description

Wheat tortillas with 7.2 g of protein each. Every pack contains 8 wraps of 40 g, flexible and easy to roll, for burritos, fajitas, quesadillas or cold wraps to take away. With 18 g of protein and 6.1 g of fibre per 100 g, they are a practical way to make quick meals more complete.

Ingredients and allergens

Wheat flour (43%), water, protein blend (19%) (wheat, pea, rice), rapeseed oil, humectant: glycerol; linseed flour, oat fibre, quinoa flour, dextrose, emulsifier: mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids; iodised table salt (salt, potassium iodate), acidity regulators: sodium acetates, malic acid.

Allergens: Contains cereals containing gluten and products thereof.

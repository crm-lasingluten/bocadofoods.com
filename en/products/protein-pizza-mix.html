Description

Protein pizza dough to make at home: 27.3 g of protein and 13.4 g of fibre per 100 g of baked dough. One bag makes several thin, crispy bases to top with your favourite ingredients.

Ingredients and allergens

Protein blend (35%) (wheat protein, sunflower seed flour, soya protein), wholemeal wheat flour (17%), soya semolina (12%), wheat bran (10%), flax seeds (7%), soya flour (6%), linseed flour (5%), oat fibre (5%), salt (<2%), emulsifier: soya lecithin (<2%); acidity regulator: sodium diacetate (<2%).

Allergens: Contains gluten from wheat and oats, and soya. May contain traces of barley, rye, spelt, egg, milk, hazelnuts, sesame and lupin.

Directions
  1. 500 g of mix + 15 g of yeast + 415 ml of water at 30 °C. Mix and leave to rest for 10 min.
  2. Divide into 150–180 g portions, shape into balls and rest for another 10 min.
  3. Flatten to 2–3 mm, prick with a fork and rest for 15 min.
  4. Pre-bake for 5 min, add the toppings and bake at 190–200 °C for 16–20 min.

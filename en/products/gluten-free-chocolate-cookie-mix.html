Description

A mix for gluten-free chocolate cookies. Homemade cookies with a classic taste, for an afternoon snack or with your coffee.

Ingredients and allergens

Sugar (58%), starch (28%) (potato starch, maize starch), fat-reduced cocoa powder (5%), modified starch (4%), emulsifier (<2%): mono- and diglycerides and polyglycerol esters of fatty acids E 471 and E 475; palm fat powder (<2%) (palm oil, glucose syrup, milk protein), protein-enriched whey powder (<2%), raising agents (<2%): disodium diphosphate E 450 (i), sodium hydrogen carbonate E 500 (ii); thickener (<2%): xanthan gum E 415; flavouring (<2%), salt (<2%).

Allergens: Contains milk. May contain traces of egg and soya. Gluten-free: gluten content below 20 ppm (Regulation (EU) No 828/2014), suitable for people with coeliac disease.

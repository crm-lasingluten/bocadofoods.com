Description

A mix for baking protein rolls and baguettes at home. With 500 g of mix, yeast and water you can make your own, in the size you prefer, with 27.3 g of protein and 13.4 g of fibre per 100 g of finished product.

Ingredients and allergens

Protein blend (35%) (wheat protein, sunflower seed flour, soya protein), wholemeal wheat flour (17%), soya semolina (12%), wheat bran (10%), flax seeds (7%), soya flour (6%), linseed flour (5%), oat fibre (5%), salt (<2%), emulsifier: soya lecithin (<2%); acidity regulator: sodium diacetate (<2%).

Allergens: Contains gluten from wheat and oats, and soya. May contain traces of barley, rye, spelt, egg, milk, hazelnuts, sesame and lupin.

Directions
  1. 500 g of mix + 15 g of yeast + 415 ml of water at 30 °C.
  2. Knead for 6 min on low speed and 10 min on high speed (dough at 26–32 °C).
  3. Divide: 50 g rolls or 110 g baguettes. Prove for 45 min covered with cling film.
  4. Bake at 230 °C, then lower to 200 °C and bake for 20–25 min.

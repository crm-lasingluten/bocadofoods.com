Description

Crunchy seed crackers with 26 g of protein and 12 g of fibre per 100 g. All you need is water and an oven: spread the mix on a tray and in 16 minutes you have a base for dips, cheese, hummus or snacking.

Ingredients and allergens

Sunflower seeds (22%), linseed (22%), wheat gluten (12%), oat flakes (11%), sesame (6%), dried glucose syrup (5%), wheat bran (5%), palm fat powder (5%) (palm oil, glucose syrup, milk protein), milk protein (4%), wheat starch (2%), malted barley flour (<2%), canola oil (<2%), salt (<2%), glucose (<2%), dried rye sourdough, yeast extract (<2%).

Allergens: Contains gluten, sesame and milk. May contain traces of lupin, soya and hazelnut.

Directions
  1. 500 g of mix + 200 ml of water at 30 °C.
  2. Line a tray with baking paper and spray it well with water.
  3. Sprinkle the mix evenly and spray with water again until moist.
  4. Bake for about 16 min at 160 °C. Break into pieces and serve.

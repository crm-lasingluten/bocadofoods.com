Description

A mix for making protein burger buns, with 29 g of protein per 100 g of finished product. 1 kg makes about 25 buns of 75 g, soft and golden, perfect for homemade meat or veggie burgers.

Ingredients and allergens

Protein blend (35%) (wheat protein, soya protein, lupin protein), soya flour, wheat flour, wholemeal wheat flour, soya semolina, soya bran, sugar, oat fibre, salt, emulsifier: sunflower lecithin; acidity regulator: sodium diacetate; inactive yeast.

Allergens: Contains gluten from wheat and oats, soya and lupin. May contain traces of barley, rye, spelt, egg and milk.

Directions
  1. 1 kg of mix + 20 g of yeast + about 900 ml of water.
  2. Knead for 10 min on speed 1 and 8–15 min on speed 2. Dough at 26–28 °C.
  3. Shape into balls of about 75 g, flatten slightly and prove for 35–45 min.
  4. Top with sesame, linseed or sunflower seeds and bake at 230 °C for 20–24 min.

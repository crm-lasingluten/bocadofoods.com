Cookie policy

This is a courtesy translation. In the event of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

Last updated: October 2026.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. This policy explains the cookies on the website www.bocadofoods.com, in accordance with Article 22(2) of the LSSI-CE and the Spanish Data Protection Agency’s Guide on the use of cookies.

Owner: BAUER LEMANS, S.L., NIF B66336264.

2. Cookies used on this website

This website does not set its own cookies or any analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts, photos and other resources are served from our own domain, without cookies.

The only exception is the YouTube videos that appear in some recipes. To protect your privacy:

  • The video does not load until you press the play button. Until then, YouTube receives no data.
  • When you press it, the video loads from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com). When it plays, Google may store or read information in your browser, according to its own policy.

3. Online shop

The shop shop.bocadofoods.com is a separate site, with its own cookie policy and its own consent notice.

4. How to manage or delete cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647
  • Mozilla Firefox: support.mozilla.org
  • Safari: support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471
  • Microsoft Edge: support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge

5. Changes to this policy

If in the future we add other cookies (for example, visitor analytics), we will update this policy and ask for your prior consent through a notice, with options to accept, reject or configure cookies just as easily.
